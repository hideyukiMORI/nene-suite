## Decisions & findings

- The signature `iat` location was resolved by reading the landed corpus (it lives in the JWS
  protected header, epoch seconds) — no Origin clarification was needed after all.
- Because the corpus had already landed, parity was tested against the **real signed corpus**
  directly rather than hand-rolled dev fixtures.
- Installed product versions are not tracked yet, so updates are surfaced **latest-only with
  `status: unknown`** (agreed) — the verified latest version is shown without claiming a diff.
- The embedded trust anchor loads from a configured path; unset means the Origin client stays
  disabled (production root keys are a human-gated ceremony).
